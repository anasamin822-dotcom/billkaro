# 01 - Requirements

Source: Sankar Group front-end assignment (Internshala chat, Nov 2026). Due 15 Nov 2026.

Must have:
- Responsive SaaS landing page (existing or invented product)
- Nav bar: logo, section links, sign up / get started button
- Hero: headline, short description, CTA, visual
- At least 3 features as cards/blocks
- About or how-it-works section
- Pricing with at least 2 plans
- Contact / footer with a demo-request or contact form
- Nav links scroll to sections, buttons do something, form validates and shows success
- No backend
- Works on mobile and desktop

Submission: live link (preferred) or local instructions, source on GitHub or ZIP,
README, 2-3 screenshots, 5-8 lines on design choices.

Product chosen: BillKaro, an invented invoicing SaaS for Indian freelancers.
Invented over existing: no brand guidelines to copy, and easy to explain in an interview.

Marked done against the PDF on 11 Nov. Anything not listed above is extra, not required.
